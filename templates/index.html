Builder of Dispatch

Dispatch is mission control for coding agents. Tasks are markdown files that live in your repo and sync through git. Each agent runs in an isolated worktree, limited to the paths its task declares, with budget caps and verify gates. It's local-first and built with Tauri, Rust, TypeScript and Bun.
